How it works
What is a proportion?

In mathematics, a proportion is the equality between two ratios. In other words, it is a relation that establishes an equivalence between four quantities, called terms, which are proportional to each other.

Fundamental property of proportions

The product of the means is equal to the product of the extremes: a × d = b × c

Example of a proportion

20:10 = 40:20 is a proportion because both ratios are equal to 2.

How to solve a proportion
  1. Identify the four terms
  2. Multiply the means
  3. Multiply the extremes
  4. Verify that the products are equal
How to find the unknown term

If you know three terms, you can find the fourth using the formula: d = (b × c) / a

Frequently asked questions
When is a proportion correct?

When the two ratios have the same value.

What are proportions used for?

They are used to compare quantities and solve mathematical and real-world problems.

How do you verify a proportion?

By multiplying means and extremes and comparing the results.
